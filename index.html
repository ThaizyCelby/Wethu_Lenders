How It Works

Five steps from application to payout.

1

Apply Online

Complete a short enquiry on this website. You'll be directed to WhatsApp to speak with our team.

2

Submit Your Documents

Send your South African ID, proof of income and banking details to our team via WhatsApp.

3

Affordability Assessment

We assess your application as required by the National Credit Act, including any applicable credit checks.

4

Receive a Decision

We aim to respond within 24 hours of receiving all required information.

5

Payout

If approved and once the credit agreement is signed, funds are paid into your bank account.
