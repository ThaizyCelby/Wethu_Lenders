About Us

Who We Are

A trusted micro-lending partner in the heart of Atteridgeville.

Wethu Micro Lenders is a proudly South African, NCR‑registered credit provider (NCRCP22737) based at 19 Temba Street, Atteridgeville 0125.

We exist to provide fast, honest, and affordable short‑term loans to hard‑working South Africans. Whether it's an emergency, school fees, or a temporary cash shortage, we're here to help you move forward.

Our loans range from R500 to R3000, with approval within 24 hours and no hidden costs. We believe in transparency, respect, and treating every client like family.
